Sit down, my friend. Here's the deal: The big shots want to own your life through your data. Passwords, photos, Bitcoins — secure them like you'd secure your own mother. Write that master password on paper, then lock it up tight. Encrypt your disk so not even the KGB can snoop.

## iCloud

> If you’re unable to turn on Advanced Data Protection for a certain period of time, the onscreen instructions may provide more details.

- **Choose** Apple menu \> System Settings.
- **Click** your name, then click iCloud.
- **Click** Advanced Data Protection, then click Turn On.
- **Follow** the onscreen instructions to review your recovery methods and enable Advanced Data Protection.

## Prepare Disk

> Yes, it takes a day, but good security is worth the wait.

**Open Disk Utility:** Go to 'Applications' \> 'Utilities' \> 'Disk Utility'.

**Choose Drive:** Select the drive you want to erase.

**Erase it, Comrade:** Click 'Erase', then under 'Security Options,' choose the most secure erase method. 

<Image src={image2} alt="Disk Utility dialog to erase the external drive “Backup”, with the Security Options button" />

> Mind [some errors](https://github.com/cryptomator/cryptomator/issues/2436#issuecomment-1249972294) may appear.

**Install Cryptomator:** [Download and install](https://cryptomator.org) this gem.

**Create a Vault:** Open Cryptomator, choose 'Create New Vault,' and give it a cool hacker name.

**Unlock:** Drag your files in there, and lock it when you're done.

## Passwords

- **Utilize** open-source password managers, such as [KeePassXC](https://keepassxc.org/).
- **Export** all your passwords from browsers and import them into your chosen manager.

### Export from Safari

- **Open** Safari.
- **Click** on "Preferences" \> "Passwords."
- **Enter** your Mac password to view saved passwords.
- **Select** the passwords you want to export, or select all.
- **Click** on three dots icon and choose "Export".

<Image src={image4} alt="Safari Passwords menu with options to import, export selected or export all passwords" />

### Import into KeePassXC

> Create a secure offline backup on encrypted USB drives. Keep it somewhere safe, perhaps next to your vodka stash.

- **Open** KeePassXC.
- **Click** on "Database" \> "Import" \> "CSV."
- **Navigate** to the CSV file you've created, select it, and follow the on-screen instructions to import the passwords. Match columns.

## Photos

- **Change** the storage format to keep original files on your Mac. Go to "Photos" \> "Preferences" \> "iCloud" and select "Download Originals to this Mac."
- **Open** the Photos app on your Mac.
- **Zoom out** the library so you can see all your files.
- **Select** all files (you can use the Command + A shortcut).
- **Click** "File" \> "Export" \> "Export Unmodified Original."
- **Choose** your desired format and location to export.
- **Store** copies on external hard drives, protected like the secrets of a Siberian mastermind.

<Image src={image5} alt="Photos File menu: Export Unmodified Originals for 2,950 items" />

## Music and Rest

- **Open** Music.
- **Select** all files, like gathering the family at the dinner table.
- **Click** "File" \> "Library" \> "Export Library.".
- **Click** on "File" \> " Print". In the print dialog, select "Save as PDF." They’re now protected, like the boss's reputation.
