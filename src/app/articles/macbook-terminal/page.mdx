## Terminal

Before I start, let’s clear up some terms. Your terminal has a “shell” (like zsh or bash), and an interface or terminal (like [iTerm](https://iterm2.com/) or HyperTerm). The shell is like the brain—it figures out what your commands mean. The interface is like your keyboard—it lets you talk to the shell. When you type commands, you’re typing them into the “prompt” or console.

## Starship

Next up: Starship. It’s a handy tool that makes your terminal faster and easier to use. ## Wrap-Up

We’re nearly there. Let’s finish off with a few more tips:

- **Faster Typing**: Use [zsh-autosuggestions](https://github.com/zsh-users/zsh-autosuggestions) to speed up typing commands.
- **Last Login Message**: Tired of the “last login” message? You can [remove it](https://medium.com/macoclock/how-to-remove-the-last-login-prompt-from-iterm-terminal-on-macos-8d70dea0f2e)!
- **Personal Touch**: Make your terminal yours by [customizing your computer’s name](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/mac-help/mchlp2322/mac).
- **Look and Feel**: [Choose a look for your VSCode terminal](https://code.visualstudio.com/docs/terminal/appearance) that matches your style.

Getting your terminal set up just right can make your coding quicker and more enjoyable. Whether you’re working on a mobile-friendly design, making a website accessible (following W3C/WCAG standards), or just coding up a storm in JavaScript or React. Make your terminal a best friend.
